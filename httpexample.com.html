About Sarac Studios

Sarac Studios is a Toronto-based multidisciplinary architecture and design studio helping people turn ideas into spaces, objects, and experiences.

We work across architecture, interiors, landscape, furniture, and graphic design, bringing ideas from the first sketch to something real.

Whether you have a clear vision or just an idea you’re trying to figure out, we work with you to develop it into something thoughtful, functional, and uniquely yours.

We care about the details, but we also believe design should be fun. We like experimenting with materials, pushing digital tools, and building things by hand.
